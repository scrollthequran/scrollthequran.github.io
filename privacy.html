Privacy Policy

Scroll The Quran · Effective 28 October 2026

Scroll The Quran ("the app") shows verses of the Quran one at a time. This page explains what data the app handles. If you have questions, write to scrollthequran@gmail.com.

Summary

The app has no accounts and no servers of its own. We, the developer, do not collect or receive your personal data. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some data to show those ads, as described below.

Data stored on your device

The verses you save stay only on your device. They are never sent to us. You can remove them by unsaving them, or by uninstalling the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show ads. According to Google, this SDK automatically collects and shares your IP address, product interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers (such as the advertising ID). Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Your IP address may be used to estimate your general location.

Google's practices are described in Google's data disclosure for the Mobile Ads SDK, the Google Privacy Policy, and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

We limit ads to content rated for general audiences, and we block sensitive ad categories such as sexual or dating content.

Your choices

European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: before ads load, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent tool. Where required, you can change your choices at any time from Ad privacy settings in the app's menu.

Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

iOS: the app does not ask for permission to track you, so it cannot access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Deleting your data

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. For data that Google holds, use the controls described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Third-party content

The Arabic text is from the Tanzil Project. The English translation is by Talal Itani, ClearQuran.com. Links to these sites open outside the app, and their own privacy policies apply.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
